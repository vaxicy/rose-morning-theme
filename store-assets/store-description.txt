Rose Morning — a gentle and elegant browsing experience.

Open Chrome in a quiet morning room. Rose Morning wraps your browser in cream paper, soft rose petals, and warm natural light. A minimal monochrome pastel theme built for calm, cozy, and premium everyday browsing.

Features:
- Soft warm rose palette: cream toolbar, blush frame, light rose active tab, gentle pink accents.
- Pure color theme — no images, no wallpapers, just clean elegance.
- Comfortable contrast and good readability for daily use.
- Carefully tuned tints that feel calm, cozy, and premium.

Let your browser breathe with the quiet warmth of a rose morning.
